3 years. 472,871 website sessions. 32,313 teddy bear orders.

I analyzed the Maven Fuzzy Factory e-commerce dataset end-to-end with SQL + Python to answer 4 questions: where does revenue come from, is the website converting, and what actually moved the needle?

Here's what the data told me 🧸

📈 1 | Growth wasn't just traffic — it was conversion.
Sessions grew 15x (1.9k → 29.7k/month), but the real story: session-to-order conversion nearly TRIPLED, from 3.2% to 8.7%. The site got dramatically better at selling.

🔍 2 | Google non-brand search drove 60% of sessions and $1.12M of the $1.94M revenue. But the most interesting insight? Brand campaigns converted 7.5–8.9% vs 6.7% for non-brand — and a social media pilot converted at just 1.1% ($0.73 revenue/session vs $3.98 for search). Clear recommendation: defend brand terms, rethink social spend.

🧸 3 | Product launches, not discounts, lifted revenue per order. AOV sat frozen at $49.99 for 10 months — then every new product launch stepped it up to ~$63. Revenue per SESSION tripled ($2.34 → $4.88) thanks to better conversion + richer product mix.

📱 4 | The big opportunity: desktop converts 8.5%, mobile just 3.1% — a 2.7x gap that persisted all 3 years across every channel. Closing half of it would have meant thousands of extra orders.
