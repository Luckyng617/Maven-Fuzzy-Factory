🧸 3 years. 472,871 website sessions. 32,313 teddy bear orders. $1.94M in revenue.

I dug into the Maven Fuzzy Factory dataset — a toy e-commerce business — to find out what actually moved the needle. Full SQL + Python project, with an interactive dashboard so you can slice it yourself (link in comments).

What the data told me:

📈 1 | Growth wasn't just traffic — it was conversion.
Sessions grew 15x, but the real story: session-to-order conversion nearly tripled, from 3.2% → 8.7%. The website got dramatically better at selling.

🔍 2 | Paid search carried the business — but brand was smarter.
Google non-brand search drove 60% of sessions and $1.12M of revenue at 6.7% conversion. Brand campaigns converted at 7.5–8.9%, and direct traffic at 7.3%. Meanwhile, a social media pilot converted at just 1.1% ($0.73 per session vs $3.98 for search). Clear verdict: defend brand terms, rethink social.

🧸 3 | Product launches, not discounts, lifted AOV 26%.
Revenue per order sat frozen at $49.99 for 10 months — then every new launch stepped it up toward $63, and revenue per session tripled ($2.34 → $4.88).

📱 4 | The big miss: mobile.
Desktop converts at 8.5%, mobile at 3.1% — a 2.7x gap that persisted all 3 years across every channel. That's the opportunity I'd chase first.
